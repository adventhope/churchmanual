# Nominating Committee

## Purpose of the Role

The Nominating Committee identifies, vets, and recommends candidates for all elected positions in the Church of the Advent Hope. This committee is one of the most consequential bodies in the church's life — the quality of its work directly shapes the quality of the church's leadership.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Identify qualified candidates** — Prayerfully seek out members who are gifted, available, and spiritually ready for each role.
2. **Vet candidates thoroughly** — Confirm willingness, qualifications, and any potential conflicts before presenting nominees to the membership.
3. **Present a complete slate** — Ensure that every elected position has a nominee; do not leave roles vacant by default.
4. **Communicate clearly** — Present the slate to the membership with enough information for members to make an informed vote.
5. **Keep role descriptions current** — Maintain and update position descriptions in coordination with the Church Manual to ensure nominees understand what they are accepting.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Incorporate Workflow Responsibility into Role Descriptions
- For Q2 2026 and beyond, ensure that all relevant position descriptions include explicit reference to the check-in workflow responsibilities tied to the thematic goal.
- When presenting nominees for positions such as Connect Team Leader, Office Manager, Small Group Leader, and Adult Sabbath School Teacher, confirm that nominees are aware of and willing to fulfill the check-in and attendance tracking expectations.

---

## Committee Composition

Per the Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual:

- The Nominating Committee is typically elected by the church at a business meeting.
- The Senior Pastor serves as an ex officio member.
- The committee should represent the breadth of the congregation — different ages, backgrounds, and ministry areas.

---

## Process

1. The committee meets prior to the annual election cycle, typically in the fall.
2. Members pray together and review all positions to be filled.
3. Candidates are contacted privately to confirm willingness and qualifications.
4. A complete slate is brought to the church membership for vote.
5. The committee remains available to fill vacancies that arise mid-term.

---

## Qualifications (Committee Members)

- Baptized members of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Broad knowledge of the congregation's membership
- Discreet — committee deliberations are confidential until the slate is presented
- Impartial — no personal agenda regarding specific positions or candidates

---

## Appointment

The Nominating Committee itself is elected by the church membership at a duly called business meeting, typically at the beginning of a new election cycle.
