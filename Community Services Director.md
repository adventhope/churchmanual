# Community Services Director

## Purpose of the Role

The Community Services Director leads the church's organized outreach and service ministry to the surrounding community. This role embodies the church's mission to serve — providing practical assistance to those in need, building relationships across community lines, and demonstrating the Gospel through action.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Develop and lead service programs** — Organize and oversee ongoing community outreach efforts such as food distribution, clothing drives, tutoring, or other local needs.
2. **Mobilize volunteers** — Recruit, train, and coordinate church members and community volunteers for service projects.
3. **Build community relationships** — Cultivate partnerships with local organizations, nonprofits, and neighborhood leaders.
4. **Communicate the ministry** — Report regularly to the congregation and board on the scope and impact of community service work.
5. **Record participation** — Track volunteer hours and participation in service events and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Track Participation Across All Gatherings
- Record attendance at all community service events in the shared church database using the canonical format established by the Office Manager.
- Ensure that community participants who are also church attendees are correctly identified and matched — do not create duplicate records.

### Serve the Community in Some Capacity (SOO for All Leaders)
- The Community Services Director models and cultivates the church-wide value of community engagement, providing accessible on-ramps for members to fulfill their own service obligations.

---

## Qualifications

- Compassionate, organized, and mission-driven
- Experience in community organizing, social services, or volunteer management preferred
- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Ability to work across cultural and socioeconomic differences with respect and humility

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
