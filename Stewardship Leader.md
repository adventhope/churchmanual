# Stewardship Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Stewardship Leader cultivates a culture of generosity and faithful resource management within the congregation. This role educates members on the biblical principles of stewardship — time, talent, and treasure — and supports the financial health of the church by encouraging consistent and generous giving.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Lead stewardship education** — Provide regular teaching, workshops, or sermon series on the biblical basis for giving, tithing, and financial stewardship.
2. **Promote consistent giving** — Encourage all members to make giving a regular spiritual practice, not only a response to special appeals.
3. **Support financial campaigns** — Assist the Treasurer and board in communicating the church's financial needs and goals to the congregation.
4. **Coordinate with the Treasurer** — Align stewardship messaging with the church's actual financial position and budget needs.
5. **Model generosity** — Live out the values of stewardship personally, as one of the Standard Operating Objectives for all church leaders.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives Alignment

Per the Advent Hope Playbook, all leaders are expected to **financially support the mission** of the church. The Stewardship Leader embodies this expectation and creates the conditions for others to do the same.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Comfortable speaking about money, giving, and financial topics in a church context
- Personally committed to faithful stewardship
- Collaborative with the Treasurer and Church Board

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
