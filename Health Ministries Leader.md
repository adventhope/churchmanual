# Health Ministries Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Health Ministries Leader equips the congregation to pursue holistic well-being — physical, mental, and spiritual — as an expression of stewardship of the body and a form of witness to the community. Health is a core dimension of the Adventist mission and an accessible bridge for community engagement.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Lead health education programming** — Organize classes, seminars, or workshops on topics such as nutrition, lifestyle, mental health, and disease prevention.
2. **Coordinate health screenings and events** — Plan and execute periodic community health events such as blood pressure screenings, cooking demonstrations, or stop-smoking programs.
3. **Collaborate with community partners** — Build relationships with local healthcare providers, clinics, or health nonprofits that can enhance the church's health ministry.
4. **Integrate health and faith** — Frame health programming within the church's Gospel mission and values — serving the whole person as an act of love.
5. **Track participation** — Record attendance at health events and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Background or strong interest in health, wellness, or healthcare (clinical credentials a plus but not required)
- Organized and capable of coordinating community-facing events
- Reflects the church's values of welcome, inclusion, and service

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
