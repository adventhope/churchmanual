# Children's Sabbath School Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Children's Sabbath School Leader oversees the Sabbath School ministry for children, ensuring that each age group has an appropriate curriculum, a trained teacher, and a consistent weekly experience. This role coordinates the full children's Sabbath School department and supports the individual teachers within it.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Coordinate the children's Sabbath School department** — Oversee all children's divisions (Kindergarten, Primary, Junior, etc.) and ensure each class is staffed and prepared each week.
2. **Support and develop teachers** — Provide resources, encouragement, and training to Children's Sabbath School Teachers.
3. **Maintain curriculum** — Select or approve curriculum for each division in alignment with the church's values and any conference guidelines.
4. **Communicate with families** — Keep parents informed about programming, curriculum themes, and special events.
5. **Record and submit attendance** — Ensure that attendance for all children's Sabbath School classes is recorded and submitted to the shared church database each week.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Track Participation Across All Gatherings
- Collect attendance records from each Children's Sabbath School Teacher each week.
- Submit consolidated children's Sabbath School attendance to the shared church database using the format established by the Office Manager.
- Ensure no division is submitting attendance through a separate, non-integrated system.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Experience in children's education or ministry
- All individuals working directly with children must comply with the church's background check and child safety policy
- Organized and able to coordinate a team of teachers

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
