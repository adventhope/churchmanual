# Communication Representative

## Purpose of the Role

The Communication Representative manages the church's internal and external communications across all channels — bulletin, email, social media, website, and other platforms. This role ensures that the congregation is informed, that visitors can easily learn about the church, and that digital engagement channels feed accurately into the church's database.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Manage the church bulletin** — Produce and distribute the weekly worship bulletin on time and accurately.
2. **Manage digital channels** — Maintain the church website, email list, and social media accounts with current and relevant content.
3. **Coordinate announcements** — Collect, edit, and disseminate announcements from ministry leaders to the congregation.
4. **Grow digital engagement** — Increase online subscribers, social media followers, and digital touchpoints as part of the church's communication strategy.
5. **Protect brand consistency** — Ensure that all church communications reflect the church's mission, values, and visual identity.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Ensure Every Person Is Captured Correctly
- Ensure that all digital sign-up forms, event registrations, and subscription links feed into the same church database used for check-in.
- Coordinate with the Office Manager to confirm that online contacts are being correctly matched to existing records or created as new entries — no parallel contact lists.

### Support Participation Tracking
- When promoting events or gatherings digitally, include registration or RSVP links that route to the official database.
- Report digital engagement metrics (website traffic, subscriptions, followers) to the Office Manager for inclusion in the monthly participation report.

---

## Qualifications

- Strong written communication skills
- Familiarity with email marketing platforms, social media, and basic web content management
- Organized and able to meet weekly deadlines
- Member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership or board. Term is typically one year with renewal.
