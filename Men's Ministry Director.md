# Men's Ministry Director

## Purpose of the Role

The Men's Ministry Director leads programs and community spaces designed to nurture the spiritual growth, fellowship, and service of men in the congregation. The role creates environments where men are challenged to grow in their faith, their relationships, and their engagement with the mission of the church and the needs of their community.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Lead men's ministry programming** — Organize regular gatherings, service projects, small groups, or study series for men.
2. **Cultivate brotherhood and accountability** — Foster a culture where men are known, supported, and challenged to live out their faith with integrity.
3. **Connect men to service** — Create pathways for men to serve in the church and community, consistent with the church-wide value of community engagement.
4. **Collaborate with other ministries** — Coordinate with the Family Ministries Coordinator, Community Services Director, and Small Group leaders to serve men holistically.
5. **Track participation** — Record attendance at men's ministry events and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Demonstrated spiritual maturity and genuine investment in other men's growth
- Organized, relational, and proactive
- Willingness to model vulnerability and servant leadership

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
