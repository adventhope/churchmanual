# Church Clerk

## Purpose of the Role

The Church Clerk maintains the official records of the Church of the Advent Hope. This role is the keeper of the church's institutional memory — documenting decisions, tracking membership, recording minutes, and ensuring that the church's legal and denominational record-keeping obligations are met.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Record and distribute board minutes** — Take accurate minutes at all Church Board meetings and distribute approved minutes to board members and the file.
2. **Maintain membership records** — Keep an accurate, current list of all baptized members, including transfers, removals, deaths, and new additions.
3. **Process membership changes** — Record baptisms, transfers in, transfers out, and other membership status changes in coordination with the pastoral team and conference.
4. **Submit required reports** — Prepare and submit the Annual Statistical Report to the conference and any other required denominational reports.
5. **Archive church documents** — Maintain organized, accessible archives of board minutes, membership records, official correspondence, and governing documents.

---

## Relationship to the Office Manager

The Church Clerk and Office Manager serve complementary but distinct functions. The Church Clerk holds the official denominational and governance records; the Office Manager manages the operational database, communications, and day-to-day administration. These roles should coordinate to ensure consistency between the official membership list and the operational database.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Organized, detail-oriented, and discreet with confidential information
- Comfortable with record-keeping systems and written communication
- Reliable attendance at board meetings

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal. The Church Clerk may also serve as an ex officio member of the Church Board.
