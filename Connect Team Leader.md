# Connect Team Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Connect Team Leader oversees the welcome and connection experience at every gathering. This role is the primary owner of the check-in workflow and is responsible for ensuring that every person who attends — new or returning — is warmly welcomed, correctly identified, and properly recorded in the church database.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Own the check-in workflow** — Maintain and continually improve the canonical check-in process for all gatherings.
2. **Train and equip volunteers** — Recruit, train, and schedule volunteers to staff check-in at every gathering type.
3. **Serve as the go-to resource** — Be available to answer workflow questions from any ministry leader or volunteer.
4. **Follow up with new visitors** — Ensure that every first-time visitor receives a follow-up contact within the week.
5. **Report regularly** — Communicate check-in data, trends, and unresolved issues to the Office Manager and pastoral team.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

The Q2 2026 thematic goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation. The Connect Team Leader is the central role in executing this goal.

### Eliminate Friction at Check-in
- Implement the canonical check-in flow at every gathering — Worship, Sabbath School, small groups, and events.
- Surface friction points to the Office Manager promptly; never allow workarounds to persist.

### Ensure Every Person Is Captured Correctly
- Train all check-in volunteers on the new-person and returning-person protocols.
- Establish a clear escalation path for ambiguous matches rather than allowing duplicates.

### Track Participation Across All Gatherings
- Verify that all ministry leaders are submitting attendance records to the shared system.
- Follow up within 48 hours when a ministry fails to submit attendance.

### Clarify the Workflow for Staff and Leaders
- Serve as the primary trainer for all staff and lay leaders on the check-in workflow.
- Maintain written workflow documentation in coordination with the Office Manager.

### Establish Shared Accountability
- Flag unresolved issues to the Office Manager or overseeing Elder within 48 hours of any gathering.
- Participate in the monthly review of participation reports.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Relational, organized, and comfortable leading volunteers
- Familiar with (or willing to learn) the church's database and check-in software
- Reliable presence at Worship and other regular gatherings

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term and reporting structure determined by the church board.
