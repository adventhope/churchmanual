# Financial Policy Manual

## Purpose of This Document

The Financial Policy Manual establishes the policies and procedures that govern the Church of the Advent Hope's handling of financial resources. These policies exist to protect the church, its leaders, and its members by ensuring that all funds are managed with transparency, integrity, and accountability.

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **Stewardship** — All financial resources are held in trust for God's mission. The church handles them with the care and accountability that trust requires.
2. **Transparency** — Financial information is available to the membership in summary form and to the board in full detail.
3. **Accountability** — No individual acts alone in handling or approving the use of church funds.
4. **Compliance** — The church follows all applicable laws, denominational guidelines, and conference requirements.

---

## Budget

- The annual budget is prepared by the Treasurer in consultation with the pastoral team and ministry leaders.
- The draft budget is reviewed and approved by the Church Board before the start of each fiscal year.
- Significant mid-year expenditures outside the approved budget require board approval.

---

## Financial Controls

### Offering Collection
- Offerings are counted by at least two unrelated individuals immediately after each service.
- Counted offerings are documented on an official count sheet signed by both counters.
- Funds are deposited by the next business day.

### Expenditure Authorization
- Routine expenditures within the approved budget may be authorized by the Treasurer.
- Expenditures above a board-defined threshold require dual authorization (Treasurer + one additional officer or board member).
- No single individual may both authorize and receive payment for the same expenditure.

### Check Signing
- Checks above a board-defined threshold require two authorized signatures.
- The Senior Pastor may not be a sole check signer.

### Credit and Purchasing Cards
- Any church-issued card must be authorized by the board.
- Monthly statements are reviewed and approved by the Treasurer and a board officer.

---

## Financial Reporting

- The Treasurer presents a financial report at each Church Board meeting.
- An annual financial summary is presented to the church membership at the annual business meeting.
- The church undergoes a periodic internal or external audit as determined by the board.

---

## Designated Funds

- Designated gifts (given for a specific purpose) must be used only for that purpose.
- If a designated fund is no longer needed, the donor must be consulted before funds are redirected.
- Designated fund balances are tracked separately and reported to the board.

---

## Tithe and Conference Remittance

- All tithe collected by the church is remitted in full to the conference on the schedule they require.
- The Treasurer is responsible for timely remittance and maintains records of all conference transactions.

---

## Conflicts of Interest

- Board members and officers must disclose any financial relationship with vendors, contractors, or recipients of church funds.
- A disclosed conflict requires the affected person to recuse themselves from related decisions.

---

## Policy Review

This document is reviewed annually by the Church Board. Amendments require board approval.
