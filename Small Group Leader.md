# Small Group Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Small Group Leader facilitates a regular gathering of church members and/or community participants for the purpose of spiritual growth, mutual support, and community building. Small groups are a primary vehicle through which the church's mission of nurturing holistic spiritual growth is embodied in the rhythms of everyday life.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Facilitate regular meetings** — Lead a small group that meets consistently (weekly or biweekly) with a clear purpose and format.
2. **Create a welcoming environment** — Cultivate a safe, inclusive space where participants feel known, heard, and challenged to grow.
3. **Connect participants to the broader church** — Help small group members feel connected to and invested in the life of the wider congregation.
4. **Develop emerging leaders** — Identify participants who could lead or co-lead a small group and invest in their development.
5. **Communicate with church leadership** — Maintain regular contact with the overseeing Elder or pastoral staff regarding the group's health and needs.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

The Q2 2026 thematic goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation.

### Track Participation
- Record small group attendance in the shared church database after each meeting.
- Use the canonical check-in flow established by the Office Manager — no local spreadsheets or workarounds.
- Submit attendance records by the established deadline each week.

### Capture New Participants
- Follow the new-person protocol when someone attends for the first time: collect contact information and submit it to the Office Manager or Connect Team Leader using the established handoff process.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing (or approved community participant for outreach-oriented groups)
- Demonstrated spiritual maturity and relational warmth
- Consistent and reliable in attendance and communication
- Willing to be trained and to operate within the church's shared systems

---

## Appointment

Small Group Leaders are identified and approved by the pastoral team and/or overseeing Elder. They are recognized by the church and listed in the church directory. Term is ongoing with annual review.
