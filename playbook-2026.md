# Advent Hope Playbook 2026

*Source: Advent Hope Playbook (For board and staff). Last modified Feb 12, 2026.*

---

## 1. Why do we exist? (Our Purpose)

To live in loving, worshiping relationship with God and in loving community with all members of the human family.

---

## 2. How do we behave? (Our Values)

**Essential Values and Practices**
- Being Christ-centered
- Being Biblical
- Welcoming and affirming all people as children of God and brothers and sisters in the human family
- Being anti-racist
- Being gracious and nonjudgmental
- Serving our community

**Aspirational Values and Practices**
- Creating safe spaces for dialogue more consistently and for more people
- Celebrating and empowering varying levels of commitment
- Intentionally communicating and connecting with new people
- Cultivating a culture of service
- Expanding engagement beyond Sabbath morning worship

**Strong Values and Practices**
- Encouraging people to be themselves while embracing communal identity
- Facilitating varied learning opportunities
- Cultivating an inclusive community reflecting NYC's diverse cultures

**Core Values and Practices**
- Being inclusive and accessible
- Strong in mutually respectful collaborative lay leadership
- Having integrity
- Being innovative
- Being a community of fully engaged people

---

## 3. What do we do? (Our Mission)

We cultivate a Gospel community of people who learn, teach, celebrate, serve, grow in Christ, and experience holistic transformation in our lives and world. The Holy Spirit empowers us to participate in God's reconciling and restorative work through Jesus Christ. (2 Corinthians 5:18-21)

---

## 4. How will we succeed? (Our Strategy)

We nurture holistic spiritual growth, communicate the Good News, and embody the life and teachings of Jesus.

**Strategy Metrics**

| Pillar | How We Measure It |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Nurture** | Qualitative interviews, surveys, and focus groups |
| **Communicate** | # small groups, worship services/attendance, Bible studies, Christian Ed sessions, online traffic, outreach events, subscriptions, followers |
| **Embody** | # volunteers in service projects, giving/stewardship, volunteering, leading, sharing, helping |

---

## 5. What is most important right now? (Thematic Goal Q2 2026)

**Fix and clarify the existing check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation.**

**Defining Objectives**

1. **Eliminate friction at check-in** — every gathering uses the same flow with no workarounds.
2. **Ensure every person is captured correctly** — new people reliably created, returning people correctly matched, no duplicates.
3. **Track participation across all gatherings** — Worship, Sabbath School, small groups, and events all record attendance in the same system.
4. **Clarify the workflow for staff and leaders** — all staff and key lay leaders can articulate the workflow and their role without ambiguity.
5. **Establish shared accountability** — ownership, handoffs, and follow-up responsibilities clearly assigned and practiced.

**Standard Operating Objectives for Deacons**

1. Participate in the life of the church community
2. Recruit and develop volunteers
3. Pursue personal spiritual development
4. Financially support the mission
5. Serve the community in some capacity

---

## 6. Who will do what to accomplish the Thematic Goal?

The goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow across all gatherings. The responsibilities below map each Defining Objective to the roles accountable for executing it. Specific assignees (staff and lay leaders) should be confirmed at the June strategic planning session.

### Recurring Calendar Items (All Roles)
1. Attend the strategic planning session in June
2. Plan ministry calendar with check-in workflow integrated as a standing agenda item

### Responsibility Matrix by Defining Objective

#### Objective 1 — Eliminate friction at check-in
*Every gathering uses the same flow with no workarounds.*

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Office Manager | Define and document the single canonical check-in flow; maintain it as the source of truth |
| Connect Team Leader | Implement the flow at every gathering; surface friction points to the Office Manager |
| Deacons & Deaconesses | Execute check-in at Worship service using the canonical flow; report problems immediately |
| Small Group Leaders | Use the same flow for small group check-in; no local workarounds |
| Elders | Reinforce consistent use of the flow across all ministries they oversee |

#### Objective 2 — Ensure every person is captured correctly
*New people reliably created, returning people correctly matched, no duplicates.*

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Office Manager | Own the database; establish and communicate the matching/deduplication protocol; audit records regularly |
| Connect Team Leader | Train volunteers on the correct new-person and returning-person workflow |
| Deacons & Deaconesses | Follow the matching protocol at Worship check-in; escalate ambiguous cases rather than creating duplicates |
| Hospitality Team | Collect new visitor information and hand off to Connect Team using the established protocol |
| Communication Representative | Ensure digital sign-ups and subscriptions feed into the same database |

#### Objective 3 — Track participation across all gatherings
*Worship, Sabbath School, small groups, and events all record attendance in the same system.*

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Office Manager | Configure the database to receive attendance from all gathering types; produce participation reports |
| Adult Sabbath School Teacher | Record Sabbath School attendance in the shared system after each session |
| Children's Sabbath School Leader | Record Children's Sabbath School attendance in the shared system |
| Small Group Leader | Record small group attendance in the shared system after each meeting |
| Community Services Director | Record community service event attendance in the shared system |
| Social Committee | Record event attendance in the shared system |
| Connect Team Leader | Verify that all gathering types are consistently submitting records; follow up on gaps |

#### Objective 4 — Clarify the workflow for staff and leaders
*All staff and key lay leaders can articulate the workflow and their role without ambiguity.*

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Office Manager | Create and distribute written workflow documentation; lead a training session before Q2 begins |
| Elders | Ensure every ministry under their oversight has received training; confirm comprehension |
| Nominating Committee | Incorporate check-in workflow responsibility into role descriptions for all future nominees |
| Connect Team Leader | Serve as the go-to resource for workflow questions from volunteers and leaders |

#### Objective 5 — Establish shared accountability
*Ownership, handoffs, and follow-up responsibilities clearly assigned and practiced.*

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Church Board Member | Review participation and check-in metrics at each board meeting; hold leadership accountable |
| Office Manager | Produce and distribute a monthly participation report to the board and pastoral team |
| Elders | Conduct quarterly check-ins with ministry leaders on workflow compliance |
| Connect Team Leader | Flag unresolved issues to the Office Manager or Elder within 48 hours of a gathering |
| Deacons & Deaconesses | Report attendance data to the Connect Team Leader or Office Manager by end of each Sabbath |

---

*Note: Section 6 should be reviewed and assignees confirmed by name at the June 2026 strategic planning session. The responsibility matrix above reflects role-level ownership; the board and staff should use that session to assign named individuals and finalize handoff protocols.*
