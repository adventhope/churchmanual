# Church Board Member

## Purpose of the Role

The Church Board is the primary governance body of the Church of the Advent Hope. Board Members share collective responsibility for the overall spiritual health, financial integrity, strategic direction, and operational accountability of the church. The Board works in partnership with the pastoral staff to fulfill the church's mission.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Attend and participate in board meetings** — Be present and engaged at all regularly scheduled board meetings; review materials in advance.
2. **Exercise faithful governance** — Make decisions in the best interest of the church's mission and membership, not personal interests.
3. **Hold leadership accountable** — Ensure that staff and ministry leaders are operating in alignment with board-approved strategy, policies, and budget.
4. **Protect the church's financial integrity** — Approve and monitor the annual budget; review financial reports; ensure compliance with the Financial Policy Manual.
5. **Support the pastoral team** — Provide oversight and pastoral care for the staff in their work, not micromanagement.
6. **Represent the congregation** — Serve as a bridge between the broader membership and the board's decisions and direction.

---

## Qualifications

Per the Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual, Board Members should:

- Be baptized members of the church in regular standing
- Have demonstrated judgment, integrity, and commitment to the church's mission
- Be free from conflicts of interest that would impair their governance role
- Be willing to give time and attention to the board's work

---

## Appointment

Church Board Members are nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. The board composition and term length are determined in accordance with the Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual and any conference requirements.
