# Women's Ministries Coordinator

## Purpose of the Role

The Women's Ministries Coordinator leads programs, gatherings, and support structures for the women of Advent Hope. This role cultivates a community where women are spiritually challenged, relationally supported, and empowered to use their gifts in the church and the world.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Lead women's ministry programming** — Organize regular gatherings, retreats, small groups, or study series for women.
2. **Foster community among women** — Create spaces where women are known, encouraged, and able to support one another through life's varied seasons.
3. **Connect women to service and leadership** — Identify and develop women's gifts for ministry, leadership, and community service.
4. **Collaborate with other ministries** — Coordinate with the Family Ministries Coordinator, Small Group leaders, and Community Services Director to serve women holistically.
5. **Track participation** — Record attendance at women's ministry events and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Demonstrated spiritual maturity and investment in other women's growth
- Organized, relational, and proactive
- Connected to the broader Adventist Women's Ministries network as a resource

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
