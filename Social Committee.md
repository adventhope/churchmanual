# Social Committee

## Purpose of the Role

The Social Committee plans and executes church social events that strengthen community bonds, celebrate milestones, and create accessible on-ramps for new people to experience the Advent Hope community in an informal setting. Social events are a meaningful part of how the church embodies its value of welcoming and affirming all people.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Plan and execute social events** — Organize a calendar of social gatherings — potlucks, holiday celebrations, fellowship events, and special occasions — that serve a wide range of members and interests.
2. **Coordinate logistics** — Manage venue, food, setup, and communication for each event.
3. **Welcome new people** — Design events that are accessible and inviting to visitors and newcomers, not only to established members.
4. **Collaborate with other ministries** — Coordinate with other ministry leaders to avoid scheduling conflicts and to support cross-ministry participation.
5. **Record event participation** — Track attendance at social events and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Track Participation Across All Gatherings
- Record attendance at all church social events in the shared church database using the format established by the Office Manager.
- Submit attendance records by the deadline established by the Office Manager.

### Support New Visitor Integration
- When a new visitor attends a social event, collect their contact information and hand it off to the Connect Team Leader using the established protocol.

---

## Qualifications

- Organized and detail-oriented, with event planning experience preferred
- Relational and enthusiastic about hospitality
- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Able to coordinate volunteers and communicate clearly with ministry partners

---

## Appointment

The Social Committee chair is nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Committee members may be recruited directly by the chair. Term is typically one year with renewal.
