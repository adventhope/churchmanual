# Adult Sabbath School Teacher

## Purpose of the Role

The Adult Sabbath School Teacher leads a weekly Bible study class for adult members and visitors. Sabbath School is the primary weekly Christian Education gathering of the church and plays a central role in discipleship, community formation, and integrating new people into the congregation.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Prepare and facilitate engaging lessons** — Lead weekly discussion-based study of the Sabbath School quarterly or other approved curriculum.
2. **Welcome all participants** — Create an inclusive, inviting environment for members, regular attendees, and new visitors alike.
3. **Foster community** — Encourage relationships among class members through prayer, sharing, and mutual support.
4. **Record and submit attendance** — Track who attends each session and submit records to the shared church database.
5. **Communicate needs to leadership** — Inform the Sabbath School superintendent or Elder of any pastoral needs, new visitors, or concerns arising in the class.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Track Participation
- Record Sabbath School attendance in the shared church database after each session.
- Use the canonical data format established by the Office Manager — no separate spreadsheets or paper-only records.
- Submit attendance by the deadline established by the Office Manager.

### Capture New Participants
- When a new visitor attends the class, introduce them to the Connect Team Leader or Hospitality Team and ensure their information is collected using the established protocol.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Familiar with Scripture and willing to engage in regular lesson preparation
- Relational and comfortable facilitating group discussion
- Reliable weekly presence

---

## Appointment

Adult Sabbath School Teachers are nominated by the Nominating Committee and confirmed by the church membership or board. Term is typically one year with renewal.
