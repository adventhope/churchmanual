# Collegiate Ministries Coordinator

## Purpose of the Role

The Collegiate Ministries Coordinator leads the church's ministry to college and university students. Located in New York City, Advent Hope is positioned to connect with a large and transient student population. This role creates on-ramps for students to experience genuine Christian community and integrates them into the life of the church during a formative season of life.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Build relationships with students** — Actively engage with college students in and around the congregation through regular contact, social gatherings, and personal investment.
2. **Facilitate a collegiate small group or gathering** — Provide a regular, student-focused community experience (Bible study, fellowship meal, small group, etc.).
3. **Connect students to the broader church** — Help students find their place in the wider congregation — not just in a youth silo.
4. **Collaborate with Adventist Campus Ministries** — Coordinate with regional or conference-level collegiate ministry networks as appropriate.
5. **Track participation** — Record student participation in collegiate gatherings and submit records to the shared church database.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Relationally engaging with young adults; ideally with personal experience in higher education
- Familiar with the spiritual questions and cultural context of college-aged adults
- Reliable and proactive communicator

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership or approved by the board. Term is typically one year with renewal.
