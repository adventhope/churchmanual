# Hospitality Team

## Purpose of the Role

The Hospitality Team creates a welcoming environment for every person who walks through the doors of Advent Hope. Team members are the first face people see and the first voice they hear. Their warmth and attentiveness set the tone for the entire gathering experience and play a direct role in whether new visitors feel seen, welcomed, and inclined to return.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Welcome every person** — Greet all attendees — members, regular visitors, and newcomers — warmly and personally at the door and throughout the gathering.
2. **Identify and assist new visitors** — Spot first-time guests, make them feel at ease, answer their questions, and guide them through the gathering.
3. **Support the check-in process** — Direct visitors to the check-in station and assist with any needs that arise during check-in.
4. **Collect and hand off visitor information** — Gather contact information from new visitors and hand it off to the Connect Team Leader using the established protocol.
5. **Coordinate refreshments and space** — Set up and manage any hospitality elements such as welcome tables, refreshments, or connection spaces before and after gatherings.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Ensure Every Person Is Captured Correctly
- Do not attempt to enter visitor data directly into the database. Collect information on the approved form or handoff sheet and submit it to the Connect Team Leader or Office Manager by the established deadline.
- If a new visitor leaves before completing check-in, collect their information manually and submit it within 24 hours.

### Eliminate Friction at Check-in
- Direct all attendees to the check-in station. Do not allow parallel informal sign-in processes to develop.
- Alert the Connect Team Leader immediately if the check-in process is causing a bottleneck or confusion.

---

## Qualifications

- Warm, outgoing, and genuinely people-oriented
- Reliable presence at assigned gatherings
- Familiarity with the congregation and the facility
- Willing to follow the established visitor intake protocol consistently

---

## Appointment

Hospitality Team members are recruited and coordinated by the Connect Team Leader. The team lead (if designated) may be nominated by the Nominating Committee.
