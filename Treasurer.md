# Treasurer

## Purpose of the Role

The Treasurer manages the financial affairs of the Church of the Advent Hope — receiving, disbursing, recording, and reporting on all church funds. This role is a sacred stewardship: the Treasurer is entrusted with the resources that the congregation has given to advance the church's mission.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Receive and deposit all funds** — Ensure that all offerings, donations, and income are properly received, documented, and deposited in a timely manner.
2. **Process authorized expenditures** — Pay approved invoices and expenses in accordance with the Financial Policy Manual.
3. **Maintain accurate financial records** — Keep up-to-date records of all income and expenditures, organized by budget category.
4. **Report to the board** — Prepare and present a financial report at each Church Board meeting, including income, expenses, and budget variances.
5. **Manage tithe remittance** — Remit all tithe to the conference on the required schedule, maintaining records of all remittances.
6. **Coordinate the annual budget** — Lead the budget preparation process in consultation with the pastoral team, ministry leaders, and board.
7. **Ensure compliance** — Operate in full compliance with the Financial Policy Manual and all applicable laws and conference requirements.

---

## Financial Controls (Summary)

The Treasurer operates within the dual-authorization and transparency requirements of the Financial Policy Manual:

- Expenditures above the board-defined threshold require a second authorized signature.
- Monthly financial statements are available to the board.
- The Treasurer does not act as a sole signer on transactions where they also have a financial interest.

*See the [[Financial Policy Manual]] for complete policies.*

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Financial literacy — comfort with basic accounting, spreadsheets, and financial reporting
- Discretion with confidential financial information
- Trustworthy, organized, and detail-oriented
- A background in accounting, finance, or bookkeeping is strongly preferred

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal. The Treasurer typically serves as a member of the Church Board.
