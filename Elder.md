# Elder

## Purpose of the Role

Elders provide spiritual oversight and pastoral care to the congregation. Together with the pastoral team, Elders shepherd the spiritual life of the church, lead in worship, administer the sacraments, and hold the ministries of the church accountable to its mission and values. The Elder board is a foundational governance and spiritual leadership body.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Provide spiritual oversight** — Shepherd assigned members and ministry areas with prayer, counsel, and care.
2. **Lead in worship** — Participate actively in Sabbath services, including prayer, scripture reading, and occasional preaching.
3. **Administer sacraments** — Assist the pastor in communion, baptism, and other ordinances as needed.
4. **Support the pastoral team** — Collaborate with the Senior Pastor and staff on congregational care and discipleship.
5. **Hold ministries accountable** — Ensure that ministries under their oversight are operating consistently with the church's mission, values, and active strategic goals.
6. **Model spiritual maturity** — Demonstrate the values of the church in word and life.

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

The Q2 2026 thematic goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation. Elders provide oversight and accountability across ministries.

### Ensure Every Ministry Receives Training
- Confirm that all ministry leaders under their oversight have attended workflow training provided by the Office Manager.
- Follow up with any leader who missed training to ensure they receive it.

### Establish Shared Accountability
- Conduct quarterly check-ins with ministry leaders on workflow compliance and participation data.
- Review monthly participation reports and bring unresolved gaps to the Church Board.
- Receive escalated issues from the Connect Team Leader or Office Manager within 48 hours and respond accordingly.

---

## Qualifications

Per the Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual, Elder candidates should:

- Be baptized members in regular standing
- Demonstrate a mature, consistent spiritual life
- Be respected within the congregation and community
- Be willing to lead, serve, and give time to the pastoral responsibilities of the role

---

## Appointment

Elders are nominated by the Nominating Committee, elected by the church membership, and ordained (or commissioned) by the conference. Term length is determined by the Nominating Committee's recommendation to the board, typically one to two years with renewal.
