# Children's Church Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Children's Church Leader oversees an age-appropriate worship and learning experience for children during the Sabbath morning service. This role ensures that children are not simply accommodated while adults worship, but are given their own meaningful encounter with God through story, song, craft, and community.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Plan and lead weekly Children's Church** — Prepare and deliver an engaging, age-appropriate program for children during Sabbath morning worship.
2. **Recruit and coordinate volunteers** — Build and manage a team of teachers, helpers, and storytellers who can share the responsibility week to week.
3. **Maintain a safe environment** — Ensure that all volunteers follow the church's child safety guidelines and that the children's space is safe and welcoming.
4. **Communicate with families** — Keep parents and guardians informed about programming, curriculum, and any events or changes.
5. **Integrate children into the broader church community** — Create regular touchpoints where children participate in the full life of the congregation, not only in their own program.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Genuine love for children and experience in child education or ministry
- All volunteers working directly with children must comply with the church's background check and child safety policy
- Organized, reliable, and collaborative

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
