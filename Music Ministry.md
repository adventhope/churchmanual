# Music Ministry

## Purpose of the Role

The Music Ministry leads the congregation in musical worship, preparing and presenting music that draws hearts toward God, reflects the church's theology and values, and serves the diverse cultural expressions of the Advent Hope community. Music is central to the worship experience and to the formation of community identity.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Lead congregational worship** — Prepare and lead music for all Sabbath morning services, special services, and other gatherings as requested.
2. **Develop and coordinate musicians** — Recruit, rehearse, and schedule vocalists, instrumentalists, and other music contributors.
3. **Curate a diverse musical repertoire** — Select music that honors the multicultural identity of the congregation and serves the varying spiritual needs of the community.
4. **Collaborate with the pastoral team** — Coordinate with the preaching pastor to ensure that music supports and reinforces the sermon theme and worship flow.
5. **Steward musical resources** — Maintain instruments, sheet music, sound systems, and other musical assets responsibly.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Demonstrated musical ability (vocal or instrumental) and worship leadership experience
- Collaborative, humble, and spiritually grounded
- Familiar with the musical traditions and preferences of a diverse urban congregation

---

## Appointment

The Music Ministry director or worship leader is nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership or appointed by the board (for staff positions). Term is typically one year with renewal.
