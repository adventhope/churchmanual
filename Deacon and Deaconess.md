# Deacon and Deaconess

## Purpose of the Role

Deacons and Deaconesses serve the practical and relational needs of the congregation, freeing pastors and elders to focus on spiritual leadership and teaching. This role is grounded in the New Testament model of servant leadership and is essential to the week-to-week health of the church community.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

All Deacons and Deaconesses are expected to fulfill the following objectives:

1. **Participate in the life of the church community** — Regular attendance at Worship, Sabbath School, and other church gatherings. Active presence is foundational to effective service.
2. **Recruit and develop volunteers** — Identify, invite, and train new volunteers to serve alongside you. Build a bench so ministry can continue without depending on any single person.
3. **Pursue personal spiritual development** — Engage in regular prayer, Bible study, and other spiritual disciplines. You cannot sustain service to others without tending to your own spiritual life.
4. **Financially support the mission** — Participate in the stewardship of the church through regular giving as an expression of commitment to the community's shared mission.
5. **Serve the community in some capacity** — Extend service beyond the walls of the church. Engage in at least one outreach or community service activity per quarter.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

The Q2 2026 thematic goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation. Deacons and Deaconesses play a frontline role in achieving this goal.

### Check-in Workflow
- Execute check-in at Worship service using the canonical flow established by the Office Manager. No local workarounds.
- Follow the matching protocol for returning visitors and members. When a match is uncertain, escalate to the Connect Team Leader rather than creating a duplicate record.
- Submit attendance data to the Connect Team Leader or Office Manager by the end of each Sabbath.

### New Visitor Hospitality
- Welcome new visitors warmly and direct them through the check-in process.
- Collect contact information for new visitors and hand off to the Connect Team using the established protocol.
- Report any friction or confusion in the check-in process immediately to the Connect Team Leader.

---

## Qualifications

Candidates for Deacon or Deaconess should:

- Be baptized members of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Demonstrate a servant heart and genuine care for people
- Be reliable and available to serve consistently
- Be willing to be trained and to train others

---

## Appointment

Deacons and Deaconesses are nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership for a term determined by the board. The role description is reviewed annually in coordination with the Playbook and any active thematic goals.
