# Community Services Volunteer

## Purpose of the Role

Community Services Volunteers carry out the hands-on work of the church's outreach and service ministry. They are the people doing the serving — staffing food distributions, participating in service days, building relationships with community members, and representing Advent Hope in the neighborhood.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Participate consistently** — Show up reliably for assigned service events and shifts.
2. **Serve with respect and humility** — Treat every person encountered in service with dignity, regardless of background or circumstance.
3. **Follow the leadership of the Community Services Director** — Take direction from the Director and communicate proactively about availability and any issues encountered.
4. **Recruit others** — Invite fellow church members and friends to participate in service projects.
5. **Reflect the church's values** — Embody the church's commitment to being anti-racist, gracious, and nonjudgmental in all service interactions.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

- Ensure that any new community contacts made during service are referred to the Community Services Director for proper entry into the church database — do not independently add contacts.

---

## Qualifications

- Open to all church members and friends, including those not yet baptized
- Willing to follow safety and conduct guidelines for each service context
- Reliable and communicative

---

## Appointment

Community Services Volunteers are recruited by the Community Services Director. No formal election required.
