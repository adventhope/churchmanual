# Office Manager

## Purpose of the Role

The Office Manager maintains the administrative and operational infrastructure of the church. This role is the owner of the member database, the primary producer of participation data, and the source of truth for all administrative policies and procedures. The Office Manager enables every ministry to function efficiently by providing reliable systems, records, and communication support.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Maintain the member database** — Keep member and visitor records accurate, current, and free of duplicates.
2. **Support ministry operations** — Provide administrative support to ministry leaders and staff as needed.
3. **Communicate regularly** — Produce and distribute the church bulletin, announcements, and other regular communications.
4. **Manage office systems** — Oversee scheduling, correspondence, filing, and operational logistics of the church office.
5. **Report on participation** — Produce regular reports on attendance and participation for the board and pastoral team.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

The Q2 2026 thematic goal is to fix and clarify the check-in workflow so every gathering consistently integrates people into the database and tracks participation. The Office Manager is the foundational role for this goal.

### Eliminate Friction at Check-in
- Define and document the single canonical check-in flow and distribute it to all ministry leaders.
- Maintain the check-in documentation as the source of truth; update it as the workflow evolves.

### Ensure Every Person Is Captured Correctly
- Own the member database and establish the official matching and deduplication protocol.
- Conduct regular database audits to identify and resolve duplicate or incomplete records.
- Communicate the protocol clearly to all volunteers and ministry leaders.

### Track Participation Across All Gatherings
- Configure the database to receive attendance submissions from all gathering types.
- Establish a consistent format and deadline for attendance submissions from each ministry.

### Clarify the Workflow for Staff and Leaders
- Create written workflow documentation and lead a training session before Q2 begins.
- Serve as the administrative authority on workflow questions.

### Establish Shared Accountability
- Produce and distribute a monthly participation report to the board and pastoral team.
- Flag ministries that are not submitting attendance records to the overseeing Elder.

---

## Qualifications

- Strong organizational and administrative skills
- Proficiency with database software, spreadsheets, and office applications
- Discretion in handling sensitive member information
- Reliable availability during office hours and around major gatherings

---

## Appointment

The Office Manager is a staff position appointed by the Senior Pastor and/or Church Board. Terms and compensation are determined by the board.
