# Religious Liberty Leader

## Purpose of the Role

The Religious Liberty Leader represents the church's commitment to freedom of conscience, separation of church and state, and the protection of religious liberty for people of all faiths and none. This role educates the congregation on religious liberty issues, engages with relevant public policy, and connects Advent Hope to the broader Adventist network of religious liberty advocacy.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Educate the congregation** — Provide regular teaching, newsletters, or programs on religious liberty principles, current issues, and the Adventist tradition of religious freedom advocacy.
2. **Monitor relevant legislation and policy** — Stay informed about local, state, and federal developments that affect religious liberty, including both protections and threats.
3. **Represent the church** — Engage with the North American Division's Department of Public Affairs and Religious Liberty (PARL) and the Adventist-Laymen's Services & Industries (ASI) networks as appropriate.
4. **Advocate respectfully** — When appropriate, engage public officials or community forums on religious liberty issues in ways that reflect the church's values of grace and inclusion.
5. **Communicate with the board** — Report annually to the Church Board on significant religious liberty developments and the church's engagement.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Interest in law, policy, and civil liberties
- Articulate, credible, and able to represent the church in public settings
- Familiar with the Adventist tradition of religious liberty advocacy

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
