# Children's Sabbath School Teacher

## Purpose of the Role

The Children's Sabbath School Teacher leads a weekly Bible lesson and worship time for an assigned age group of children. This role shapes the early faith formation of the next generation and creates an environment where children feel loved, safe, and excited about learning.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Prepare and lead weekly lessons** — Deliver an age-appropriate, engaging lesson each Sabbath based on the approved curriculum.
2. **Welcome children warmly** — Create a consistent, nurturing environment where every child feels known and valued.
3. **Maintain safety standards** — Follow the church's child safety and supervision guidelines at all times.
4. **Record and report attendance** — Take attendance each week and submit it to the Children's Sabbath School Leader by the established deadline.
5. **Communicate needs** — Inform the Children's Sabbath School Leader of any pastoral concerns, supply needs, or issues in the class.

---

## Responsibilities Related to the Q2 2026 Thematic Goal

### Track Participation
- Take attendance at every class session and submit it to the Children's Sabbath School Leader — not directly to the database — using the approved form or digital method.

---

## Qualifications

- Member of the Church of the Advent Hope (baptized members preferred; others by pastoral approval)
- A heart for children and ability to communicate with them at their level
- Must comply with the church's background check and child safety policy
- Reliable weekly presence

---

## Appointment

Children's Sabbath School Teachers are recruited and approved by the Children's Sabbath School Leader in coordination with the Nominating Committee.
