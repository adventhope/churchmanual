# Family Ministries Coordinator

## Purpose of the Role

The Family Ministries Coordinator leads programming and support for families at every stage — couples, parents of young children, blended families, and multigenerational households. The goal is to help families thrive spiritually and relationally, and to ensure the church is a resource and community for them beyond Sabbath morning.

---

## Standard Operating Objectives

1. **Develop family-focused programming** — Plan and lead events, workshops, small groups, or series that address the needs of families in the congregation.
2. **Support marriage and parenting** — Provide accessible resources, referrals, and community for couples and parents.
3. **Coordinate with other ministries** — Collaborate with Children's Church, Sabbath School, and Small Group leaders to ensure families are served holistically.
4. **Connect families to the broader church** — Help families, especially those with young children, feel integrated into the full life of the congregation — not only in family-specific programming.
5. **Communicate regularly** — Keep families informed about programming and communicate needs to the pastoral team and board.

---

## Qualifications

- Baptized member of the Church of the Advent Hope in good standing
- Demonstrated commitment to family health and pastoral sensitivity
- Organized, warm, and collaborative
- Experience in family ministry, counseling, or education preferred

---

## Appointment

Nominated by the Nominating Committee and elected by the church membership. Term is typically one year with renewal.
